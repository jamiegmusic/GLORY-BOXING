## 1. Core Game Loop & Mechanics

### Daily Management Cycle (Real-time pausable)
- **Morning Brief (8 AM)**: Review overnight social media activity, press coverage, and urgent communications
- **Work Hours (9 AM - 6 PM)**: 
  - Schedule meetings with talent
  - Review project proposals
  - Manage ongoing productions
  - Handle media requests
  - Training sessions
- **Evening Review (6 PM - 10 PM)**: 
  - Industry events and networking
  - Social media strategy
  - Personal development time
- **Late Night (10 PM - 2 AM)**: Crisis management window for breaking scandals

### Weekly Management Tasks
- **Monday**: Financial review and budget allocation
- **Tuesday**: Talent scouting reports and recruitment meetings  
- **Wednesday**: Project planning and pitch meetings
- **Thursday**: Media and PR strategy sessions
- **Friday**: Contract negotiations and legal matters
- **Weekend**: Industry events, premieres, award shows

### Seasonal Cycles
- **Pilot Season (January-April)**: TV show auditions and commitments
- **Summer Blockbusters (May-August)**: Major film productions
- **Award Campaign (September-December)**: Oscar/Grammy push
- **Holiday Season**: Brand partnerships and special appearances

### Talent Acquisition Framework
1. **Scouting Network**
   - Junior scouts: $50K/year, cover local markets
   - Senior scouts: $150K/year, national coverage
   - International scouts: $250K/year, global talent search
   - Digital scouts: $100K/year, social media talent identification

2. **Signing Process**
   - Initial contact and interest gauge (1-7 days)
   - Preliminary negotiations (3-14 days)
   - Contract structuring (7-21 days)
   - Legal review and signing (3-7 days)

3. **Development Pathways**
   - **Fast Track**: High-risk, high-reward aggressive booking
   - **Steady Build**: Conservative project selection for consistent growth
   - **Reinvention**: Complete image/career overhaul for established talent
   - **Crossover**: Transitioning between entertainment mediums

## 2. Celebrity Attribute System

- **Charisma** (45-100): Natural magnetism, likability, star quality
- **Intelligence** (40-100): Script analysis, business acumen, adaptability
- **Work Ethic** (35-100): Punctuality, preparation, stamina
- **Professionalism** (40-100): Set behavior, media training, industry relationships
- **Emotional Stability** (30-100): Stress management, consistency, reliability

**Media Attributes**
- **Social Media Savvy** (20-100): Platform understanding, content creation, engagement
- **Interview Skills** (35-100): Soundbites, charm offensive, damage control
- **Fashion Sense** (30-100): Red carpet impact, brand alignment, trendsetting
- **Controversy Resistance** (25-100): Scandal avoidance, PR recovery, public perception

**Business Attributes**
- **Negotiation Power** (30-100): Deal-making, leverage understanding, timing
- **Brand Value** (35-100): Endorsement potential, merchandise sales, licensing
- **International Appeal** (20-100): Global marketability, cultural adaptability
- **Longevity Potential** (40-100): Career sustainability, reinvention capability

1. **Training Methods**
   - Acting coaches: +0.5-2.0 Acting/month ($5K-50K/month)

2. **Experience Gains**
   - Lead film role: +2-5 Acting, +1-3 Charisma

   - Live performance: +1-4 Stage Presence, +1-2 Emotional Stability

3. **Life Event Modifiers**
   - Marriage: +5 Emotional Stability, +3 Public Image
   - Divorce: -8 Emotional Stability, -5 Controversy Resistance
   - Award Win: +10 Negotiation Power, +7 Industry Reputation

## 3. Project Management System

### Project Categories

**Film Productions**
- **Indie Films**: Budget $500K-$5M, 3-6 week shoots, high critical potential
- **Studio Films**: Budget $20M-$100M, 8-16 week shoots, mainstream appeal
- **Blockbusters**: Budget $100M+, 16-32 week shoots, franchise potential
- **Streaming Originals**: Budget $10M-$50M, 6-12 week shoots, algorithm-driven

**Television Projects**
- **Network Shows**: 22-24 episodes, $2M-$5M/episode, 9-month commitment
- **Cable Dramas**: 8-13 episodes, $3M-$8M/episode, 6-month commitment
- **Streaming Series**: 6-10 episodes, $5M-$15M/episode, 4-month commitment
- **Reality Shows**: Variable episodes, $500K-$2M/episode, 3-month commitment

**Music Projects**
- **Studio Albums**: 3-6 month production, $200K-$2M budget
- **Tours**: 20-150 dates, $500K-$50M gross potential
- **Collaborations**: 1-4 week commitment, revenue sharing
- **Residencies**: 3-12 month contracts, $100K-$1M per show

**Brand Partnerships**
- **Endorsements**: 1-3 year contracts, $100K-$50M value
- **Product Lines**: 18-month development, 20-50% equity stake
- **Appearances**: Single events, $10K-$500K fees
- **Social Campaigns**: 1-6 month contracts, $50K-$5M value

### Project Evaluation Matrix

Each project is evaluated on:
1. **Financial Score (0-100)**: Upfront pay, backend potential, opportunity cost
2. **Career Score (0-100)**: Prestige, skill development, industry relationships
3. **Risk Score (0-100)**: Schedule conflicts, reputation damage, physical demands
4. **Synergy Score (0-100)**: Brand alignment, cross-promotion, franchise potential

### Production Management

**Pre-Production Phase**
- Contract negotiation: 1-4 weeks
- Schedule coordination: 1-2 weeks
- Preparation/rehearsal: 2-8 weeks
- Publicity planning: 1-3 weeks

**Production Phase**
- Daily call sheets and scheduling
- Performance monitoring and feedback
- Media management and set visits
- Crisis prevention and response

**Post-Production Phase**
- ADR and reshoots: 1-2 weeks
- Marketing campaign: 4-12 weeks
- Press tour: 2-6 weeks
- Award campaign: 3-6 months

### Quality Outcome Calculations

Project Success = (Celebrity Performance × Director Quality × Script Quality × Marketing Budget) / Production Challenges

- **Critical Success**: 75+ on aggregator sites
- **Commercial Success**: 2.5x+ budget return
- **Career Impact**: +5-20 attribute points across relevant skills
- **Award Potential**: Unlocks campaign opportunities

## 4. Reputation & Media Ecosystem

- General audience perception
- Affects box office draw, merchandise sales
- Influenced by: Media coverage, social media, project choices
- Recovery rate: 0.5-2 points/week without incidents

**Industry Reputation (0-100)**
- Professional standing among peers
- Affects project offers, negotiation leverage
- Influenced by: On-set behavior, box office results, award wins
- Recovery rate: 0.3-1 point/week through good work

**Media Relationships (0-100 per outlet)**
- Individual scores with major outlets
- Affects coverage tone, exclusive access
- Influenced by: Interview access, exclusive stories, advertising spend
- Management through PR team actions

**International Reputation (0-100 per region)**
- North America, Europe, Asia, Latin America, Middle East
- Affects international project opportunities
- Influenced by: Regional projects, cultural sensitivity, touring

## 5. Crisis Management Framework

### Scandal Classification System

**Level 1: Minor Incidents (1-3 severity)**
- Social media misunderstandings
- Minor fashion faux pas
- Harmless dating rumors
- Response time: 24-48 hours

**Level 2: Moderate Scandals (4-6 severity)**
- On-set conflicts
- Controversial statements
- Relationship drama
- Response time: 6-12 hours

**Level 3: Major Crises (7-9 severity)**
- Legal accusations
- Substance abuse allegations
- Financial impropriety
- Response time: 1-3 hours

**Level 4: Career-Threatening (10 severity)**
- Criminal charges
- Major discrimination scandals
- Industry blacklisting offenses
- Response time: Immediate

### Response Strategy Framework

**Denial Strategy**
- Cost: $10K-$100K legal fees
- Success rate: 20-60% depending on evidence
- Risk: Backfire potential if proven false
- Recovery time: 2-6 months

**Apology Strategy**
- Cost: $5K-$50K PR campaign
- Success rate: 40-80% depending on sincerity
- Risk: Admission of guilt implications
- Recovery time: 1-3 months

**Deflection Strategy**
- Cost: $20K-$200K media manipulation
- Success rate: 30-70% depending on alternative story
- Risk: Transparency backlash
- Recovery time: 3-8 months

**Rehabilitation Strategy**
- Cost: $50K-$500K comprehensive program
- Success rate: 60-90% with full commitment
- Risk: Lengthy time investment
- Recovery time: 6-18 months

### Legal System Integration

**Contract Types**
- Talent Representation: 1-5 years, 10-20% commission
- Project Contracts: Per-project basis, detailed rider requirements
- Endorsement Deals: 6 months-5 years, morality clauses
- NDA Agreements: Perpetual, $100K-$10M penalties

**Legal Actions**
- Cease and Desist: $5K-$20K, 1-2 week resolution
- Defamation Suits: $50K-$500K, 6-24 month timeline
- Contract Disputes: $100K-$2M, 3-18 month resolution
- Criminal Defense: $200K-$5M, 6-36 month process

### PR Campaign Mechanics

**Campaign Elements**
1. **Media Blitz**: $50K-$500K, saturate news cycle
2. **Charity Alignment**: $20K-$200K donation + appearance fees
3. **Documentary/Interview**: $30K-$300K production, controlled narrative
4. **Social Media Campaign**: $10K-$100K, grassroots support building

**Success Metrics**
- Sentiment Analysis: -100 to +100 scale
- Media Mentions: Volume and tone tracking
- Social Media Sentiment: Real-time monitoring
- Industry Support: Peer endorsements

## 9. Specific Gameplay Scenarios

### Scenario 1: The Oscar Campaign Dilemma

**Situation**: Your client Maria Rodriguez has delivered a powerhouse performance in "Broken Chains," a small indie drama. It's October, and award season is approaching.

**Initial Context**:
- Film budget: $3M
- Current box office: $8M
- RT score: 94%
- Client stats: Acting 89, Charisma 76, Industry Rep 71
- Available campaign budget: $500K
- Main competitor: Helen Mirren in studio prestige picture

**Decision Points**:

1. **Campaign Strategy** (Choose primary focus):
   - Grassroots authentic approach ($150K)
   - Traditional FYC campaign ($300K)
   - Aggressive studio-style push ($500K)
   - Digital-first modern campaign ($200K)

2. **Screening Strategy**:
   - Film festival circuit (3 months, $50K)
   - Private industry screenings (6 weeks, $100K)
   - Public theater expansion (2 months, $200K)
   - Streaming release with events (1 month, $75K)

3. **Media Approach**:
   - Emotional personal story angle
   - Underdog narrative
   - Artistic achievement focus
   - Social issue alignment

**Consequences Tree**:

- **Path A** (Authentic + Festivals + Personal): 65% nomination chance, maintains artistic credibility, future indie opportunities, but may lack mainstream push
- **Path B** (Traditional + Industry + Achievement): 75% nomination chance, builds industry relationships, higher cost depletes other opportunities
- **Path C** (Aggressive + Theater + Social): 70% nomination chance, raises public profile significantly, risk of "trying too hard" backlash
- **Path D** (Digital + Streaming + Underdog): 55% nomination chance, builds younger fanbase, innovative approach may impress or confuse Academy

**Long-term Impacts**:
- Win: +30 Industry Rep, +50% future salary, prestige project offers
- Nomination only: +15 Industry Rep, +25% salary, quality role increase
- Campaign failure: -5 Industry Rep, -$500K sunk cost, relationship strain

### Scenario 2: The Scandal Management Crisis

**Situation**: Your biggest client, action star Jake Thunder ($20M per film), is photographed in a bar fight at 2 AM. Video shows him throwing the first punch. His $150M superhero sequel starts filming in 3 weeks.

**Initial Context**:
- Client stats: Acting 72, Emotional Stability 41, Controversy Resistance 38
- Current projects: $150M superhero sequel, $80M streaming series
- Brand deals: $30M athletic wear, $20M energy drink
- Social media: 45M followers across platforms
- Legal status: No arrest yet, but victim considering pressing charges

**Immediate Decisions** (You have 6 hours before mainstream media picks up):

1. **Legal Strategy**:
   - Preemptive settlement offer ($500K-$2M)
   - Aggressive legal defense ($200K retainer)
   - Wait and see approach
   - Victim support offering ($100K charity donation)

2. **Media Response**:
   - Complete media blackout
   - Controlled exclusive to friendly outlet
   - Full transparency press conference
   - Social media direct address

3. **Studio Communication**:
   - Immediate full disclosure to studio heads
   - Downplay severity in initial contact
   - Let legal team handle studio relations
   - Propose solutions proactively

**24-Hour Evolution**:

Based on initial choices:
- **TMZ has the video**: Severity increases by 20%
- **Victim's lawyer speaks**: Demands escalate to $5M
- **Studio threatens recast**: Must save $150M project
- **Sponsors wobble**: 48 hours to save $50M in endorsements

**Consequence Variations**:

- **Best Case** (Settlement + Exclusive + Proactive): 70% chance of maintaining all projects, -$3M total cost, -10 reputation
- **Moderate Case** (Defense + Blackout + Disclosure): 50% chance of keeping superhero role, lose one sponsor, -15 reputation
- **Worst Case** (Wait + Transparency + Downplay): 30% chance of career survival, lose all sponsors, potential jail time, -40 reputation

**Rehabilitation Options**:
- Anger management program (3 months, $50K)
- Public service campaign (6 months, $200K)
- Documentary redemption arc (1 year, $500K)
- Quiet career hiatus (6-12 months, lose momentum)

### Scenario 3: The Cross-Media Opportunity

**Situation**: Your client Emma Chen, primarily known for romantic comedies (Acting 81), receives three simultaneous offers that would define her career trajectory.

**The Offers**:

1. **Netflix Global Series**
   - $2M per episode, 10 episodes

## Implementation Notes

### Core Gameplay Principles

1. **Every Decision Matters**: No choice should be cosmetic - all options must have meaningful short and long-term consequences

2. **Interconnected Systems**: Celebrity attributes affect project outcomes, which influence reputation, which determines future opportunities

3. **Risk vs Reward**: Higher potential rewards should always come with proportional risks

4. **Time Pressure**: Many decisions must be made under deadline pressure, preventing perfect optimization

5. **Emergent Storytelling**: System interactions should create unique narratives that feel authentic to entertainment industry

### Balancing Considerations

- **Difficulty Curve**: Start with C-list client management, gradually introduce complexity
- **Money Scaling**: Early game thousands, mid game millions, late game billions
- **Random Events**: 20-30% randomness to prevent pure optimization
- **Failure Recovery**: Always possible to rebuild, but requires time and strategy
- **Multiple Paths**: Various valid strategies to reach victory conditions

### Technical Requirements

- **Simulation Speed**: Variable time progression (pause, 1x, 5x, 10x, 30x)
- **Save System**: Autosave plus unlimited manual saves
- **Database**: Track thousands of industry figures and relationships
- **AI Systems**: Dynamic news generation, realistic industry behavior
- **Mod Support**: Allow community additions of celebrities, events, projects

This design framework provides the foundation for a celebrity management simulation that matches Football Manager's depth while capturing the unique drama and complexity of the entertainment industry. The interconnected systems ensure that players face meaningful decisions with far-reaching consequences, creating emergent narratives that feel authentic to the world of celebrity management.
