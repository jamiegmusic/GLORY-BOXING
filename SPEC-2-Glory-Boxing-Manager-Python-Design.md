## 🥊 FIGHT SIMULATION & COMBAT ENGINE

### Real-Time Fight Simulation System
- **Advanced Physics Engine**: Realistic punch mechanics with velocity, angle, and impact calculations
- **Dynamic Damage System**: Cumulative damage tracking (head, body, arms, legs) with realistic injury progression
- **Stamina Management**: Real-time energy depletion based on activity level, weight cutting effects, and conditioning
- **Ring Movement AI**: Intelligent footwork patterns, corner cutting, and ring generalship
- **Defensive Mechanics**: Blocking, parrying, slipping, and rolling with realistic effectiveness rates
- **Counter-Punching System**: Timing-based counter opportunities with damage multipliers
- **Cut System**: Realistic facial damage with blood flow affecting vision and referee decisions
- **Knockdown Physics**: Authentic knockdown mechanics with mandatory 8-count and recovery assessment

### Style-Based Combat Algorithms
- **Boxer vs Brawler Dynamics**: Technical precision vs power punching with realistic advantages
- **Southpaw Advantages**: Stance-specific benefits and disadvantages in matchups
- **Switch-Hitting Mechanics**: Dynamic stance switching with timing and effectiveness penalties
- **Pressure Fighting**: Aggressive forward movement with stamina costs and psychological impact
- **Counter-Punching Specialists**: Timing-based damage bonuses and defensive bonuses
- **Body Puncher AI**: Strategic body shot targeting with cumulative damage effects
- **Head Movement Specialists**: Advanced defensive techniques with realistic success rates

### Corner Management System
- **Real-Time Corner Advice**: Dynamic strategy adjustments between rounds
- **Cut Man Effectiveness**: Skill-based cut management affecting fight continuation
- **Corner Staff Specialization**: Different trainer types (technical, motivational, defensive)
- **Round-by-Round Analysis**: Detailed statistics and strategy recommendations
- **Emergency Medical Assessment**: Real-time injury evaluation and fight stoppage decisions
- **Psychological Support**: Corner morale boosting and mental state management

### Referee & Judging Systems
- **Referee Personalities**: Different officiating styles affecting fight flow and point deductions
- **Judging Tendencies**: Individual judge preferences (clean punching, aggression, ring generalship)
- **Hometown Decisions**: Realistic bias factors in close fights
- **Point Deduction System**: Foul detection and penalty assessment
- **Fight Stoppage AI**: Medical and safety-based stoppage decisions
- **Scorecard Transparency**: Real-time scoring display with round-by-round breakdown
