## 📋 SPECIFICATION OVERVIEW

### Game Vision
**Glory Boxing Manager** is envisioned as the most comprehensive boxing management simulation ever created, developed in Python with a focus on hyper-realism, interconnected systems, and authentic boxing experience.

### Core Philosophy
- **Interconnectedness**: Every decision has cascading consequences throughout the boxing ecosystem
- **Authenticity**: Real-world boxing elements, regulations, and business practices
- **Depth**: Football Manager-level complexity with boxing-specific mechanics
- **Player Agency**: Meaningful choices that affect multiple systems simultaneously

```

Game Engine: Custom Python engine
AI Framework: Machine learning and neural networks
Graphics: 2D/3D rendering (OpenGL/DirectX)
```

1. **Fighter Management**: Recruit, develop, and manage fighters
2. **Training & Preparation**: Plan training camps and prepare for fights
3. **Fight Booking**: Negotiate contracts and schedule fights
