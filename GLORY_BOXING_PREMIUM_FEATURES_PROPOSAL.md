## 🎯 **PREMIUM FEATURE CATEGORIES**

### **1. Live Broadcast & Streaming Integration** 📺
**Feature Name:** "Glory Live" - Professional Broadcast Experience

**Core Functionality:**
- Real-time commentary feeds with professional broadcast graphics, dynamic camera angles (corner cam, ringside, overhead, crowd, drone), and live statistics overlays
- Integration with Twitch/YouTube streaming APIs for marquee fights, complete with viewer interaction and real-time audience reactions
- Multi-language commentary support with regional broadcast teams and localized content

**Player Benefit:**
- Transforms major fights into authentic TV broadcast experiences with professional production values
- Creates memorable moments through dynamic camera work and real-time audience engagement
- Enhances the prestige of championship fights and builds anticipation for marquee events

**Technical Considerations:**
- WebRTC integration for real-time streaming, WebSocket connections for live audience interaction
- GPU-accelerated graphics rendering for broadcast-quality overlays and camera transitions
- CDN integration for global content delivery and low-latency streaming
